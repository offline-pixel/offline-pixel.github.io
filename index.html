How does the hiring process work?

Submit your project requirements, discuss scope directly with an expert, agree on deliverables and timelines, fund the engagement through Offline Pixel, and release payment after approved delivery. You can speak directly with engineers before committing.

Who owns the source code and intellectual property?

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, ownership of all approved deliverables transfers to the client after full payment has been completed. Mutual NDAs can be executed before sharing sensitive business information, proprietary datasets, source code, or technical documentation.

How quickly can a project start?

Most project requests receive an initial technical review within 2 to 12 hours. Timelines depend on project complexity, scope, and resource availability. Larger projects can be split into multiple milestone-based phases for reduced risk and incremental validation.
