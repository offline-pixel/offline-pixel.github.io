Executive Summary

An investment bank's expense approvals took 2 weeks-paper forms, manual routing, and no visibility. MEAN developers built a digital workflow engine with role-based approvals, automated reminders, and audit trails, reducing approval time to 2 hours and saving $5M annually.

Key Outcomes

  • 2 weeks → 2 hours approval time
  • $5M annual cost savings
  • 10,000 approvals processed monthly

Client Situation

Expense reports required 5-7 manager signatures on paper forms. Lost forms and slow routing frustrated employees and finance teams.

Key Challenges

  • •2-week average approval cycle
  • •15% of paper forms lost annually
  • •No visibility into approval status

Existing Architecture

Paper forms, inter-office mail, manual email follow-ups, Excel tracking.

  • No digital audit trail
  • No automated reminders
  • No delegation or escalation

Solution Design

MEAN-based workflow engine with BPMN modeling, role-based routing, and automated escalations.

Key Decisions

  • BPMN for workflow modeling (50+ approval types)
  • Angular Material stepper for multi-step forms
  • Automated reminders and escalations via Node scheduler
MongoDBExpress.jsAngularNode.jsBPMNAngular Material

Implementation

Pilot for expense approvals first, then expanded to purchase orders and travel requests.

  1. Phase 1: Phase 1: Expense Workflow

    Digitized 5-step approval chain-time reduced 2 weeks → 2 days.

  2. Phase 2: Phase 2: Automation

    Added reminders, escalations, and delegation-time reduced 2 days → 2 hours.

  3. Phase 3: Phase 3: Expansion

    Added 50+ workflows for purchasing, travel, and compliance.

Technical Challenges

Dynamic approval chains per department

Impact: Hardcoded workflows didn't scale

Resolution: BPMN-driven workflow engine with runtime resolution

Integration with SAP backend

Impact: No API for legacy financial system

Resolution: Flat file exchange + scheduled sync
