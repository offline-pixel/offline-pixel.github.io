Questions before you hire?

Learn how Offline Pixel handles engineering engagements, project delivery, payments, intellectual property ownership, and specialist hiring.

What engineering expertise and support does Offline Pixel provide?

We provide access to specialists in advanced domains including Rust systems, AI infrastructure, LLM/RAG applications, quantitative engineering, and distributed systems. We support global clients through various engagement models, including hourly consulting, fixed-price projects, fractional support, and long-term contracts.

How does the engagement and communication process work?

We prioritize direct collaboration. Clients consult with experts on technical feasibility and architecture before hiring. Once requirements are scoped, you can easily manage deliverables through a milestone-based approach. We support international clients and provide an NDA-protected environment for all sensitive projects.

What are the project timelines and financial workflows?

Initial technical reviews are typically completed within 2–12 hours. Regarding payments, funds are securely held until you approve specific project milestones. For large initiatives, we use a phased delivery model, with individual engagements capped at $5,000 USD to ensure manageable, high-quality delivery.

How does Offline Pixel differ from traditional freelance marketplaces?

We move away from high-volume bidding to focus on curated engineering expertise and transparent, direct partnerships. We ensure client ownership of all intellectual property upon final payment and maintain a high standard for technical quality, security, and professional communication.
