FAQ

Common questions from engineering teams.

What trading systems can your quant developers build?

They build algorithmic trading platforms, order management systems (OMS), execution management systems (EMS), risk management systems, and portfolio management systems.

Do your quant developers handle low-latency or high-frequency trading systems?

Yes, they specialize in low-latency systems with microsecond response times, co-located infrastructure, kernel bypass, and hardware acceleration where needed.

What backtesting infrastructure do your developers build?

They build event-driven backtesting engines, vectorized backtesters, walk-forward validation frameworks, and simulation environments with realistic market impact modeling.

How do your developers handle market data?

They build real-time and historical market data pipelines, tick normalization systems, data warehouse architectures, and streaming ETL workflows.

What tech stack do your quant developers use?

Rust, C++, Python, Go, Kafka, Redis, DuckDB, ClickHouse, PostgreSQL, TimescaleDB, Kubernetes, Docker, ZeroMQ, WebSocket, and FIX protocol.

Can your developers integrate with broker APIs?

Yes, they have extensive experience with Interactive Brokers, Binance, Coinbase, Alpaca, FXCM, OANDA, and custom broker APIs.

What's the typical engagement model for quant developers?

Project-based development, ongoing infrastructure support, part-time embedded developer, or full-time dedicated quant engineer.
