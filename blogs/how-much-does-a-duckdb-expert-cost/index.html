Contract and Freelance Rates

  • •Junior DuckDB engineer: $60-90/hour
  • •Mid-level DuckDB engineer: $90-140/hour
  • •Senior DuckDB engineer: $140-200/hour
  • •DuckDB architect/consultant: $200-300/hour

    Factors That Influence Cost

    • •SQL expertise (complex queries, window functions, optimization)
    • •Data engineering experience (ETL pipelines, data modeling)
    • •Experience with Parquet and columnar formats
    • •Python/R/Julia integration skills
    • •Migration experience (SQLite → DuckDB, Postgres → DuckDB)

      Skills That Command Premium Rates

      • •Query engine internals knowledge
      • •Large-scale OLAP optimization
      • •ClickHouse, BigQuery, or Snowflake expertise
      • •Data platform architecture experience
      • •Migration leadership experience
