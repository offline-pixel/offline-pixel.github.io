Freelance and Contract Rates (Hourly)

  • •Junior mobile developer: $50-80/hour
  • •Mid-level mobile developer: $80-130/hour
  • •Senior mobile developer: $130-200/hour
  • •Lead/Architect: $180-300/hour

    Factors That Influence Cost

    • •Platform (iOS devs and Android devs cost similar; cross-platform often cheaper)
    • •Location (US and Western Europe are most expensive; Eastern Europe and Latin America are cheaper)
    • •Experience (senior devs cost 2-3x juniors but ship faster)
    • •App complexity (games vs CRUD vs AR/VR - huge range)
    • •Engagement model (full-time vs contract vs freelance)

      Cost Versus Business Impact

      Higher rates may be justified when candidates:

      • •Have shipped multiple production applications
      • •Reduce time-to-market significantly
      • •Can lead architecture decisions
      • •Improve performance and reliability
      • •Mentor junior developers
      • •Require less oversight from stakeholders

        Questions to Ask Before Setting a Budget

        Clarify these requirements first:

        • •Prototype, MVP, or enterprise application?
        • •Single platform or multiple platforms?
        • •Expected maintenance period after launch?
        • •Need for design, QA, and DevOps support?
        • •Expected user volume in the first year?
        • •Timeline constraints and launch deadlines?
