Contract and Freelance Rates

  • •Junior MEAN developer: $45-65/hour
  • •Mid-level MEAN developer: $65-95/hour
  • •Senior MEAN developer: $95-150/hour
  • •MEAN architect/consultant: $150-230/hour

    Factors Beyond Hourly Rate

    • •Ability to work independently
    • •Architecture experience
    • •Communication skills
    • •Testing and quality ownership
    • •Deployment experience
    • •Documentation quality
    • •Production troubleshooting capability

      Factors That Influence Cost

      • •Angular expertise (advanced RxJS, NgRx, Ivy)
      • •TypeScript proficiency (complex types, generics)
      • •Node.js backend depth (scaling, security, performance)
      • •Enterprise experience (large codebases, multiple teams)
      • •Testing (Jasmine, Karma, Jest, Supertest)

        Common Hiring Budget Mistakes

        • •Selecting solely on lowest rate
        • •Ignoring maintenance costs
        • •Overlooking communication ability
        • •Not validating production experience
        • •Underestimating onboarding requirements
