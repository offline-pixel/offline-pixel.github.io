    How to Interview HFT Quant Developers

    Don't ask LeetCode. Ask:

    • •Design a lock-free market data feed handler
    • •How would you optimize this hot path for branch predictability?
    • •Walk me through a race condition you debugged in production
    • •How would you measure end-to-end latency from exchange to strategy?

      How to Verify HFT Experience

      • •Ask about latency budgets they worked against
      • •Ask which exchanges or venues they integrated with
      • •Discuss production incidents they resolved
      • •Review performance profiling methodologies
      • •Evaluate understanding of market microstructure
