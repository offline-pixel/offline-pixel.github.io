You have a great alpha signal. Your backtests look amazing. Then you trade it live and the performance disappears. Welcome to execution - where 50% of alpha dies. A quant trader who understands execution is worth their weight in gold. Here's how to hire one.

Execution Algorithms Every Quant Trader Must Know

Non-negotiable knowledge:

  • •VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price) - benchmark and algo
  • •TWAP (Time-Weighted Average Price) - simple but effective
  • •Implementation Shortfall - minimizing slippage from decision to execution
  • •POV (Percentage of Volume) - trading at a fixed percentage of market volume
  • •IS (Implementation Shortfall) algos - balancing urgency with market impact

    Execution Systems Experience

    Look for experience with:

    • •Order management systems
    • •Execution management systems
    • •Exchange connectivity
    • •Real-time monitoring
    • •Post-trade analytics

      Interview Questions That Test Execution Expertise

      You need to buy 100,000 shares of a liquid stock. How do you execute?
      Great answer: Assess urgency. For low urgency, use VWAP or POV. For high urgency, use IS algo. Consider time of day, volatility, and liquidity profile.
      How do you estimate market impact?
      Look for references to Kyle's lambda, Almgren-Chriss framework, or empirical models. Senior candidates have measured impact on their own trades.
      What's the difference between VWAP and TWAP? When would you use each?
      TWAP ignores volume - trades evenly over time. VWAP matches volume profile. Use TWAP for low-volume periods or when volume forecast is unreliable. Use VWAP when you trust volume predictions.

      Red Flags in Execution Quant Traders

      Walk away if they:

      • •Only know VWAP (no TWAP, IS, or POV knowledge)
      • •Can't explain how market impact affects P&L
      • •Have never traded during volatile market conditions
      • •Don't understand exchange fee structures and rebates
      • •Have no experience with real-time monitoring of executions

        Beyond Algorithms: Implementation Skills

        Great execution quant traders also understand:

        • •Smart order routing - which exchange gets your order?
        • •Liquidity-taking vs liquidity-providing strategies
        • •Post-trade analysis - did you execute well?
        • •Real-time adjustment - algos aren't set-and-forget
        • •Broker algos vs building your own

          Execution Trader Verification Checklist

          Verify candidate can demonstrate:

          • •Live execution experience
          • •Slippage analysis capability
          • •Market impact understanding
          • •Risk control implementation
          • •Production trading system exposure
