What is MindyCore?

🎯 Our Mission

We build digital experiences designed for cognitive ease — reducing sensory load, eliminating guesswork, and creating predictable environments where neurodivergent minds can thrive.

💡 Our Approach

Every decision we make starts with a question: "Does this make the experience more accessible?" From our color choices to our navigation structure, accessibility isn't an add-on — it's our foundation.

🌟 Our Values

Clarity over cleverness. Predictability over surprise. Control over automation. Honesty over hype. These guide everything we build.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MindyCore free?

Yes! Our core features are completely free. We're committed to keeping essential tools accessible to everyone.

Will MindyPlay be available on iOS and Android?

Yes! MindyPlay will launch on both iOS and Android. We'll announce specific release dates as we get closer.

How is this different from other AI assistants?

NeuroMindy is designed specifically for neurodivergent users — with clear language, predictable responses, and no hidden features.

Can I use the accessibility tools on other websites?

These tools work on this site only, but we're building browser extensions to bring our accessibility features everywhere you go online.
