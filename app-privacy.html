4. Data Deletion Rights

In compliance with Google Play policies, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated data. You can do this within the app settings or by contacting us via the email below.

Our services are intended for students age above 18. If you are under the age of 18 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions), please ensure you have parental consent before sharing personal information.
