Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 4, 2026

Welcome to KuberQL. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the data you share with us while using our application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Device Identifiers: We collect unique device identifiers (Device ID) and operating system details to manage your account registration, prevent duplicate trials, and ensure technical stability.
  • Account Data: If you create an account, we store your email address and learning progress data to synchronize your experience across devices.
  • Usage Metrics: We track interactions within the app to improve our educational features and optimize user experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information to:

  • Authenticate your device and provide access to EdTech services.
  • Manage freemium features and premium subscription status.
  • Monitor app performance and troubleshoot technical issues.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is handled securely. Account information and study progress are stored on secure servers, while device-specific identifiers are used solely for registration and security purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal data. We may use third-party analytics or infrastructure providers (such as Google Play Services) that may collect information used to identify you. Please refer to their respective privacy policies.

5. Your Data Rights

In compliance with global privacy standards and Google Play policies, you have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your account and associated data. You can initiate a deletion request within the app settings or by contacting us via email.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it immediately.
