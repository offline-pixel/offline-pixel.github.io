Frequently Asked Questions About Commodities Quant Trading

What commodities are best for quant trading?
Energy (oil, gas) and metals have the most liquid futures markets. Agricultural commodities have strong seasonality but lower liquidity.
How do storage costs affect futures prices?
Storage costs create contango (higher future prices). Convenience yield creates backwardation. Model both for accurate pricing.
What's the capital requirement for commodities quant trading?
$5M-50M depending on commodity and position sizes. Energy markets require higher capital than grains.
