Common Mistakes When Hiring Crypto Exchange Quant Developers

Hiring general C++ developers without exchange experience

Why it happens: Order matching has unique requirements

Impact: Matching engine bugs and order fairness issues

💡Prioritize candidates with exchange or matching engine experience

Ignoring lock-free data structures

Why it happens: Mutexes are easier to implement

Impact: Context switching kills performance during high load

💡Test lock-free programming knowledge specifically

Not testing deterministic execution

Why it happens: Determinism seems automatic

Impact: Matching engine non-deterministic across replays

💡Test deterministic order book reconstruction

Underestimating market data distribution

Why it happens: Focus on matching, not distribution

Impact: Market data lags behind matching

💡Design real-time distribution from day one

No 24/7 operational design

Why it happens: Assume maintenance windows

Impact: Exchange downtime during upgrades

💡Design for zero-downtime upgrades

Frequently Asked Questions About Crypto Exchange Matching Engines

Price-time priority vs pro-rata matching?
Price-time priority is most common for fairness. Pro-rata is used for some derivatives markets. Choose based on market requirements.
What's the maximum order throughput for a matching engine?
100,000-1,000,000 orders per second depending on hardware and optimization. Lock-free design scales with cores.
How to handle flash crashes in order matching?
Circuit breakers, volatility pauses, and order rate limiting. Design these into the exchange from day one.
