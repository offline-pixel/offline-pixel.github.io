Frequently Asked Questions About FAISS in Bioinformatics

FAISS vs BLAST for sequence similarity?
BLAST for exact sequence matching. FAISS for approximate learned embeddings. Use both for different use cases.
What's the cost of FAISS for bioinformatics?
$5k-50k monthly for GPU instances depending on dataset size. Academic discounts available on cloud platforms.
How to validate biological similarity results?
Domain expert review and benchmark against known structures. FAISS recall should exceed 95% for research use.
