Common Mistakes When Hiring HFT Quant Developers

Hiring general C++ developers without HFT experience

Why it happens: HFT has unique requirements (latency, FIX, lock-free)

Impact: Systems too slow to be competitive

💡Prioritize candidates with HFT or low-latency experience

Ignoring FIX protocol expertise

Why it happens: Developers assume exchange APIs are simple

Impact: Order rejection and missed trades

💡Test FIX protocol knowledge specifically

Not testing lock-free programming knowledge

Why it happens: Concurrency is complex

Impact: Data races and order corruption

💡Ask about atomic operations, memory ordering, and CAS

Underestimating kernel bypass importance

Why it happens: Standard network stacks seem sufficient

Impact: Latency disadvantage of 10-100 microseconds

💡Prioritize candidates with DPDK or Solarflare experience

Hiring without performance testing

Why it happens: Resumes don't measure latency

Impact: Unknown performance until production

💡Include latency benchmarks in interview process

Before You Hire an HFT Quant Developer

  • Define latency targets (microseconds or nanoseconds)
  • Select exchange connectivity method (FIX, proprietary)
  • Choose kernel bypass technology (DPDK, Solarflare)
  • Design lock-free data structures for order book
  • Plan for hardware selection (FPGA, low-latency NICs)

Frequently Asked Questions About HFT Quant Developers

C++ or Rust for HFT systems?
Both are used. C++ dominates legacy systems. Rust is gaining adoption for new systems due to memory safety without performance loss. Choose based on team expertise and existing codebase.
What's the compensation for HFT quant developers?
$300k-1M+ total compensation including bonuses. HFT quant developers are among the highest-paid engineers due to direct P&L impact.
How long does it take to build an HFT system?
6-18 months for production-ready systems with experienced quant developers. Exchange connectivity, market data handlers, and risk systems are complex.
