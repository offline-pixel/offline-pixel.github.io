Common Mistakes When Hiring Prop Trading Quant Traders

Hiring researchers without trading experience

Why it happens: Research and execution require different skills

Impact: Poor execution and missed opportunities

💡Prefer candidates with trading experience

Ignoring risk management

Why it happens: Focus on returns, not risk

Impact: Large drawdowns and firm losses

💡Test risk management knowledge specifically

No position sizing discipline

Why it happens: Intuitive sizing seems sufficient

Impact: Overbetting during winning streaks

💡Require systematic position sizing

Strategy overconcentration

Why it happens: Researchers focus on one strategy

Impact: Correlated losses

💡Diversify across uncorrelated strategies

Hiring without drawdown experience

Why it happens: Everyone performs in bull markets

Impact: Poor performance during drawdowns

💡Ask about drawdown management
