Common Mistakes When Hiring LLM Engineers for Healthcare

Hiring LLM engineers without healthcare domain knowledge

Why it happens: Clinical documentation has unique requirements

Impact: Inaccurate medical entity extraction and coding errors

💡Prioritize candidates with clinical NLP or healthcare AI experience

Not implementing de-identification

Why it happens: Developers underestimate HIPAA requirements

Impact: PHI exposure and compliance violations

💡Implement HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identification

Using generic LLMs for clinical text

Why it happens: General models are easier to deploy

Impact: Poor medical entity recognition and clinical accuracy

💡Fine-tune on clinical data or use medical-specific models

Ignoring EHR integration

Why it happens: Developers focus on LLM, not workflow

Impact: System doesn't fit clinical workflow and is abandoned

💡Design for EHR integration from day one

No clinical evaluation

Why it happens: Developers use generic NLP metrics

Impact: System performs poorly on real clinical notes

💡Evaluate with clinicians on realistic test data

Frequently Asked Questions About LLM Clinical Documentation

Can LLMs be used for clinical documentation under HIPAA?
Yes, with proper safeguards: BAAs with cloud providers, on-premise deployment, or private cloud. Data must be encrypted at rest and in transit, with access controls and audit logs. Many healthcare organizations use LLMs in HIPAA-compliant environments.
What's the cost to build a clinical documentation system?
$150k-400k depending on EHR integration, feature requirements, and compliance needs. HIPAA compliance adds 30-50% to development costs.
How accurate are LLMs for clinical text?
Fine-tuned medical LLMs achieve 90-95% accuracy on clinical entity extraction, comparable to human coders. However, human review is still recommended for high-stakes decisions.
