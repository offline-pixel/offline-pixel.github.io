Frequently Asked Questions About Low-Latency Systems

DPDK vs Solarflare vs io_uring?
DPDK most common for HFT. Solarflare is vendor-specific. io_uring is newer Linux option. Choose based on exchange connectivity and team expertise.
What's the achievable latency with kernel bypass?
500ns-5μs for network I/O, depending on hardware and optimization. Without kernel bypass, 10-50μs.
Do you need FPGAs for low latency?
Not initially. C++/Rust with kernel bypass achieves sub-10μs. FPGAs add 100ns-1μs but require specialized skills.
