Common Mistakes When Hiring Healthcare RAG Engineers

Hiring RAG engineers without medical domain knowledge

Why it happens: Medical terminology is specialized

Impact: Poor retrieval accuracy

💡Prioritize candidates with medical NLP experience

No PHI de-identification

Why it happens: Assume standard RAG is sufficient

Impact: HIPAA compliance failure

💡Implement Safe Harbor de-identification

Not using medical ontologies

Why it happens: Generic embeddings

Impact: Missed medical concepts

💡Use UMLS or SNOMED CT

No audit logging for research access

Why it happens: Assume internal only

Impact: Compliance risk

💡Log all PHI access

Poor clinical note chunking

Why it happens: Generic document chunking

Impact: Missing clinical context

💡Chunk by clinical section

Before You Hire a Healthcare RAG Engineer
