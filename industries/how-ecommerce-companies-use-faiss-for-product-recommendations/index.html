Frequently Asked Questions About FAISS for E-commerce

HNSW vs IVF for product recommendations?
HNSW for higher recall (95%+). IVF for higher throughput (10,000+ QPS). Choose based on latency requirements.
How to generate product embeddings?
Collaborative filtering from user interactions, or product metadata transformers. Both combined works best.
What's the cost of FAISS for e-commerce?
$5k-50k monthly for GPU instances depending on index size and QPS. CPU-only is cheaper but slower.
