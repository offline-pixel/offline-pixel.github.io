Common Mistakes When Hiring LLM Engineers for Legal

Hiring LLM engineers without legal domain knowledge

Why it happens: Legal has unique requirements (citations, accuracy, privilege)

Impact: Hallucinations and missing critical legal context

💡Prioritize candidates with legal tech or compliance experience

Ignoring hallucination detection

Why it happens: LLMs naturally hallucinate without mitigation

Impact: Incorrect legal advice and malpractice risk

💡Implement RAG with citation tracking and confidence scoring

Poor document chunking

Why it happens: Generic chunking doesn't work for legal documents

Impact: Missing context and inaccurate answers

💡Chunk based on document structure (clauses, sections, paragraphs)

No citation tracking

Why it happens: Developers focus on answers, not provenance

Impact: Unable to verify answers or audit results

💡Track source documents for every generated statement

Underestimating document volume

Why it happens: Legal discovery can involve millions of documents

Impact: Scaling and performance issues

💡Design for large-scale vector search and batch processing

Frequently Asked Questions About LLM Legal Document Analysis

Can LLMs be trusted for legal document review?
With proper RAG architecture, citation tracking, and hallucination detection, LLMs can significantly accelerate legal document review. However, human oversight is still recommended for high-stakes decisions. Many law firms use LLMs for first-pass review with attorney verification.
What's the cost to build a legal document analysis system?
$100k-300k depending on document volume, feature requirements, and compliance needs. RAG systems with citation tracking cost more but provide auditability essential for legal use.
How does attorney-client privilege apply to LLM document analysis?
LLM systems must be deployed in a privileged environment with access controls, audit logs, and data isolation. Cloud LLM APIs may not be appropriate for privileged documents. On-premise or private cloud deployment is often required.
