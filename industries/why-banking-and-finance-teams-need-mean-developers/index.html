Common Mistakes When Hiring Banking MEAN Developers

Hiring without banking security knowledge

Why it happens: Banking has unique requirements

Impact: Security vulnerabilities

💡Prioritize candidates with fintech or banking experience

No audit logging

Why it happens: Assume internal logs sufficient

Impact: Compliance failure

💡Log all data access and modifications

Weak authentication

Why it happens: Assume simple JWT sufficient

Impact: Account takeover risk

💡Implement refresh tokens and MFA

No input validation

Why it happens: Assume frontend validation only

Impact: Data corruption risk

💡Validate all inputs on backend

No role-based UI

Why it happens: Assume API auth sufficient

Impact: Information leakage

💡Hide unauthorized UI elements

Frequently Asked Questions About MEAN stack under Banking

Is MEAN secure enough for banking?
Yes, with proper implementation. Use HTTPS, JWT with refresh tokens, MFA, and comprehensive audit logging.
How to achieve compliance with MEAN?
Log all data access, implement RBAC, validate all inputs, and use encryption for sensitive data.
What's the cost of a banking internal system?
$150k-500k depending on features. Security and compliance add 30-50% to development costs.
