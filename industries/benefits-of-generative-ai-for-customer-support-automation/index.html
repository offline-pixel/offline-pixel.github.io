Common Mistakes When Hiring LLM Engineers for Customer Support

Hiring LLM engineers without support domain knowledge

Why it happens: Support has unique requirements (citations, confidence, handoff)

Impact: Untrustworthy answers and poor customer experience

💡Prioritize candidates with conversational AI or support experience

No citation tracking

Why it happens: Developers focus on answers, not provenance

Impact: Customers can't verify answers or trust the system

💡Track source documents for every answer

Ignoring confidence scoring

Why it happens: LLMs are always confident even when wrong

Impact: Customers receive incorrect answers

💡Implement confidence thresholds and agent handoff for low confidence

Poor knowledge base integration

Why it happens: Knowledge bases are often disorganized

Impact: RAG retrieval fails to find relevant answers

💡Clean and structure knowledge base before RAG implementation

No human-in-the-loop for improvement

Why it happens: Developers assume LLMs work perfectly out of the box

Impact: System doesn't improve over time

💡Log failures and use for fine-tuning or prompt improvement

Frequently Asked Questions About Generative AI Support

Can LLMs replace human support agents?
Not entirely. LLMs excel at answering common questions from knowledge bases, resolving 30-50% of tickets. Complex issues, unhappy customers, and sensitive situations still require human empathy and judgment. The best approach is AI-first with seamless agent handoff.
What's the cost of running an LLM support chatbot?
Costs vary by volume and model choice. Expect $0.01-0.10 per conversation depending on token usage. Caching common questions reduces costs significantly. For 10,000 monthly conversations, budget $100-1,000 for LLM API costs.
How accurate are LLM support answers?
With well-structured knowledge bases and RAG, accuracy exceeds 90-95% for common questions. Citation tracking allows customers to verify answers. Uncommon questions should trigger agent handoff.
