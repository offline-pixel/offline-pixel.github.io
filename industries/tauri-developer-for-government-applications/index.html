Government Tauri Development: Questions

Can independent Tauri developers obtain security clearance?
Yes, independent contractors can obtain clearance through sponsoring government agency. Process takes 6-12 months for Secret level.
How do Tauri apps compare to Electron for FedRAMP?
Tauri has significantly smaller attack surface and fewer vulnerabilities. FedRAMP authorization typically faster due to reduced scope.
What compliance frameworks do Tauri developers support?
FedRAMP, CMMC, NIST 800-53, HIPAA, SOC2. Rust's security properties help achieve all major compliance standards.
